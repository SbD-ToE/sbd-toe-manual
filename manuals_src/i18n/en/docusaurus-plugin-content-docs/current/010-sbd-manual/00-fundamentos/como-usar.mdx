# How to Use This Manual

This manual was designed to support **technical teams, managers and decision-makers** in the practical implementation of **Security by Design** in software development.  
Its aim is to turn **good intentions into concrete and sustainable actions**, adapted to the real risk and maturity of each organisation, dispelling any notion of complexity and difficulty attached to it.
Security by Design is a path, "made by walking", and this manual teaches the steps to follow so that everyone can apply the practices. 

---

## 🎯 Who it is for {#-a-quem-se-destina}

This manual is useful for:

- Development and DevOps teams
- Software and solution architects
- Security team (AppSec, GRC, CISO)
- Product, project and quality managers
- Operations, cloud and infrastructure team

## 🧭 How to navigate {#-como-navegar}

Each chapter (01–14) follows the same pattern:

- **Summary** — Purpose and context 
- **How to Do It** — What to do, when and by whom, in the form of normalised _user stories_
- **Policies** — describes which policies must be instituted to _enforce_ the practices across the organisation
- **Technical Supplement** — Detailed explanation of the prescribed practices, with examples, tools and scenarios
- **Advanced Recommendations** — In some cases and for specialised contexts

Reading by process: the [five macro-processes](/sbd-toe/sbd-manual/fundamentos/macro-processos) describe what must remain connected across chapters — cross-cutting paths, with control points and indicators.

### 🏷️ Symbols and Icons {#️-símbolos-e-ícones}

- **⭐** — Foundational (mandatory at every level)
- **⚙️** — Operational (depends on the level and context)
- **🏛️** — Organisational (governance and compliance)
- **📚** — Technical Supplement (optional deep dive)
- **US** — User Story (activity for a specific role)

### 🔄 Proportionality (L1–L3) {#-proporcionalidade-l1l3}

SbD-ToE adapts to risk:

- **L1** — Low risk (prototypes, internal systems)
- **L2** — Moderate risk (most applications in production)
- **L3** — Critical (sensitive data, critical infrastructure)

Each chapter states what it prescribes for each level, ensuring that the prescription is proportional to the "risk" of the application.

## 🛠️ How to apply in practice {#️-como-aplicar-na-prática}

### 📘 Linear reading {#-leitura-linear}

Recommended for teams structuring processes or audits:  
Follow the natural order of the chapters (1 to 14), starting with risk management.

### 🧩 Modular reading {#-leitura-modular}

Recommended for specific initiatives (e.g. a focus on secure CI/CD):  
Allows chapter-by-chapter reading, with independent application.

## ✅ In an audit context {#-em-contexto-de-auditoria}

- Each chapter has a **verifiable checklist** with objective criteria
- The items are binary: **yes / no**
- The absence of **foundational chapters** invalidates the maturity assessment

---

## 📊 In maturity assessment {#-em-avaliação-de-maturidade}

- The contents are aligned with recognised frameworks:
  - **OWASP SAMM** - Design, Implementation and Verification domains
  - **NIST SSDF** - PO.x, RV.x and PP.x
  - **SLSA** - Levels 2 to 3 of supply chain security

- They index the resolution of common threats (ASVS, CAPEC, OSC&R, et al.)
- They respond, in the context of application development, to the emerging requirements of:
  - NIS2
  - DORA
  - CRA

---

## 🧠 In team onboarding {#-em-onboarding-de-equipas}

This manual can be used as:

- A structured introduction to security by technical domain
- A base of good practices for pipelines and operations
- Training material for Champions and mentoring programmes
