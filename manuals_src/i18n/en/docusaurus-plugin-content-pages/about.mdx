# About the SbD-ToE Project

## What It Is {#o-que-é}

**Security by Design - Theory of Everything (SbD-ToE)** is a practical and prescriptive manual for integrating security into software development, from architecture through to operation.

SbD-ToE connects:
- **Regulations** (NIS2, DORA, GDPR, ISO 27001)
- **Maturity frameworks** (SAMM, BSIMM)
- **Real threats** (MITRE ATT&CK, CWE, incidents)
- **Software engineering** (SDLC, DevOps, testing)

All of this in a coherent framework, proportional to risk and reusable.

---

## Why It Exists {#por-que-existe}

Software security is fragmented. Each organisation speaks different languages:

- Some speak compliance and regulation
- Others speak maturity and models
- Others speak threats and incidents
- Others speak development and engineering

**Nobody integrates it all.**

SbD-ToE fills that gap with a unified model that:

✅ **Is not generic** — Proportional to risk (L1, L2, L3)  
✅ **Is not theoretical** — Practical, with examples and templates  
✅ **Is not overly prescriptive** — Flexible and adaptable  
✅ **Does not duplicate** — Each practice is traceable to its origin (standard, threat, etc.)

## Who Is Behind It {#quem-está-por-trás}

SbD-ToE is the result of:

- **More than a decade** of practice in operational security
- **Security communities** (OWASP, MITRE, ENISA)
- **Continuous feedback** from real teams and organisations
- **Evolution based on incidents** and lessons learned

It is an initiative of **[ShiftLeft](https://shiftleft.pt)** and is open to the community.
