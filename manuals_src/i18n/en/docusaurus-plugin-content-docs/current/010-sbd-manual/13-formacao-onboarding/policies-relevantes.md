# Organisational Policies - Training and Secure Onboarding

The effective application of Chapter 13 - Training and Secure Onboarding - depends on the existence of **formal organisational policies** that sustain:

- Mandatory minimum training by role and by access;
- Objective validation of knowledge before permissions are granted;
- Traceability of training, even in third-party or outsourcing contexts;
- The integration of training as part of the security lifecycle (SbD).

---

## 📌 Fundamental note {#-nota-fundamental}

> ✅ Training must be treated as a **mandatory security control**, not as an option or a formality.

---

## 🧾 Recommended policies {#-políticas-recomendadas}

| Policy Name                           | Mandatory | Application                                               | Minimum expected content                                                                                           |
|--------------------------------------------|-------------|---------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| [Training by Profile Policy](/sbd-toe/assets/policies/policy-formacao-seguranca)            | Yes         | All staff members with technical roles             | Definition of training tracks by role (e.g. Dev, QA, DevOps), with minimum content, sequence and validation     |
| [Onboarding Validation Policy](/sbd-toe/assets/policies/policy-formacao-seguranca)        | Yes         | All new technical members (internal or external) | Mandatory training before technical permissions, quiz, onboarding checklist validated by the responsible team |
| [Third-Party Integration Policy](/sbd-toe/assets/policies/policy-formacao-seguranca)        | Yes         | Suppliers, contractors and external teams             | Responsibility statement, minimum training, validation quiz and record with traceability                        |
| [Training Content Management Policy](/sbd-toe/assets/policies/policy-formacao-seguranca) | Recommended | AppSec team, Champions and those responsible for training     | Modular structure by chapter of the SbD-ToE, versioned templates and artefacts applicable to projects              |
| [Training Indicators Policy](/sbd-toe/assets/policies/policy-kpis-governacao)        | Recommended | Security, quality and governance teams             | Training coverage KPIs by team, validation metrics, alerts for incomplete or outdated training   |
| [Training Update Policy](/sbd-toe/assets/policies/policy-formacao-seguranca)          | Optional    | AppSec, training team and Champions                   | Periodic review of content, update after relevant changes (e.g. incidents, changes to the SbD-ToE)       |

---

## ✅ Final recommendations {#-recomendações-finais}

- These policies must be **formally approved by security leadership** and communicated to the onboarding, training and hiring areas.
- They must be included as a **reference in contracts, SLAs, onboarding plans and continuous assessment cycles**.
- The systematic adoption of these policies makes training an **auditable measure**, with a direct impact on the organisation's security maturity and posture.

> 🛡️ A clear, applied and audited training policy is one of the most effective pillars of incident prevention.
