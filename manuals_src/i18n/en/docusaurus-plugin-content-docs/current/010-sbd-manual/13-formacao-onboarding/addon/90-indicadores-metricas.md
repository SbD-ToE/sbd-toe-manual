# Training Indicators and Metrics

These indicators help to assess whether the training programme is effective, comprehensive and up to date.

> This file is an **operational synthesis** for day-to-day use. The canonical catalogue of `TRN-Kxx` is in [`11-kpis-metricas.md`](./11-kpis-metricas.md).

---

## 🧮 Operational KPIs {#-kpis-operacionais}

| Metric                                                      | Suggested minimum target     |
|--------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| % of new members of staff who complete technical onboarding    | ≥ 95% within 7 days              |
| % of suppliers with a record of completed training          | ≥ 90%                        |
| No. of updates to the training content per year           | ≥ 2                          |
| % of PRs that follow the patterns after onboarding                  | ≥ 80%                        |
| No. of awareness sessions or labs per quarter             | ≥ 1 / team                 |
| No. of incidents with a cause linked to a lack of training        | 0 (ideal target)              |

---

## 🎯 Qualitative metrics {#-métricas-qualitativas}

- Feedback from participants (score or NPS)
- Exception requests or questions after training
- Change in behaviour (e.g. use of secrets, secure dependencies)
- Integration of training into the rituals (retro, review, sprint)

---

> 📌 These metrics must be monitored with the support of HR, AppSec and technical management.
