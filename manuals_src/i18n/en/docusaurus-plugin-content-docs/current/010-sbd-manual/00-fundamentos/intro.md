# Foundations of the Technical Manual

This chapter establishes the **conceptual and operational foundations** of *Security by Design – Theory of Everything (SbD-ToE)*.  
It defines how the manual must be read, applied and governed, and clarifies the **non-negotiable principles** that guide every chapter.

SbD-ToE **is not a catalogue of good practices**, nor an optional guide.  
It is a **prescriptive model**, proportional to risk, grounded in evidence and supported by formal mechanisms of organisational governance.

---

## 🧭 What this manual is (and is not) {#-o-que-este-manual-é-e-não-é}

**SbD-ToE is:**
- An integrated system for designing, developing, operating and governing secure software;
- A model in which technical practices, people and organisational decisions are linked;
- A manual applicable to applications, pipelines, infrastructure, teams and the relationship with suppliers.

**SbD-ToE is not:**
- A set of optional recommendations;
- A manual dependent on specific tools;
- A model based on implicit trust or on untracked local decisions.

## 🏛️ Fundamental principles {#️-princípios-fundamentais}

The following principles apply throughout the manual:

- **Security is neither optional nor local**: practices that are not applied require a formal decision.
- **Automation executes, governance authorises**: pipelines, tools and processes do not replace organisational responsibility.
- **Every exception is explicit, temporary and traceable**.
- **The lifecycle is complete**: training, onboarding, execution, continuous validation and offboarding are part of the model.
- **The manual prescribes to those who operate within its process**: in its own domain it says how to do things; in other domains of the organisation it says what must exist, not how they work; from those who deliver from outside it asks for demonstrable alignment — a supplier's obligation arises from the contract, not from this manual.

These principles are operationalised in the organisational and technical chapters of the manual.
